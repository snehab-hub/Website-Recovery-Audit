Duties:

The Planning Board reviews and acts on applications such as site plan, subdivision, special use. The Planning
Board also reviews and makes recommendations to other Boards such as the Village Board on issues such as the
Comprehensive Plan and parcel re-zoning.

FAQs:
General FAQ's
1. Regular monthly meetings are held on the 1st and 3rd Thursdays each month, at 7:00 P.M., in the Civic
Center Board Room, Potsdam, New York, except during the months of November through March, when one
meeting per month is conducted on the first Thursday of each month.
. Public Hearings and Special Meetings are scheduled when necessary.
. Members are appointed by the Mayor, subject to the approval of the Board of Trustees.
. An Oath of Office is required.
. The term for an appointment is for a five-year term, unless the appointment is made to fill the unexpired
term of another person who resigned.
